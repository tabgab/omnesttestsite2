-- a software development company, looking for an embeddable simulation kernel for your product? Have you already run into a dead-end when other products suddenly didn't have an API for what you wanted to do or when it turned out that you didn't have enough control over them. With OMNEST™ this cannot happen -- you have the source, so extending or modifying the kernel itself is a valid option.

-- the R&D department of a technology company, searching for a powerful tool to design and validate your hardware products, software architecture or communication protocol early in the development process? Is it important to you to be able to integrate the simulation tool into your existing workflow, and co-operate with all your existing software tools (regardless of what they are)? Is it important that you have a GUI assist to you in the beginning, to later bypass it when you need to automate processes? Is it important that you have all doors open to implement your ideas, including running your simulation real-time and connecting it with real-world devices or networks? You'll like OMNEST™ with its open interfaces, easy-to-process text files, plugin interfaces for input and output, handling external events -- and of course the full OMNEST™ source code which lets you implement anything we didn't think of.

-- a consulting company, in need of a discrete event simulation tool to assist your client in gaining insight into the characteristics of their systems? Do you need to map complex systems, pinpoint bottlenecks, examine fault tolerance and scalability? Do you frequently propose changes to existing architectures, or oversee the development of new systems? You will enjoy the ease with wich OMNEST™ allows you to examine various load scenarios.

-- operating a communication network, and hunting for bottlenecks and ways to improve the performance of your network? Do you need to create a detailed model of the application typical on your network? Has it ever obstructed you that your simulation software didn't provide source code for protocol models, application models or physical channel models? With OMNEST™/OMNeT++, all model sources are available to you to examine, refine or instrument with recording additional statistics. Do you have some unusual tunelling of protocols in your model? For OMNEST™/OMNeT++ this is no problem because every protocol model is an independent component and you can combine them to your liking.
