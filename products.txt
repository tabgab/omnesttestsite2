The OMNEST™/OMNeT++ answers page..

OMNEST™ is an open source simulation framework that runs on a variety of
operating systems.

OMNEST™ has been popular for a long time under the name OMNeT++.

OMNEST™ is a flexible and extensible simulation environment with strong GUI
support and an embeddable simulation kernel. OMNEST™ excels in the design
and validation of communication protocols and in architectural design and
because of its generic and flexible approach, it has been successfully used
in other areas like the simulation of IT systems, queueing networks,
hardware architectures and business processes as well.

OMNEST™ excels at several exotic scenarios where other products cannot take
you. Embedding simulations in your own product is a breeze -- and you can
do the integration at C++ level, no need to embed an OCX or launch and talk
to an external process. If you need to take input (model or parameters)
from alternative sources, or redirect output or results, OMNEST™ lets you
do it -- database integration is easily doable. Real-time or
hardware-in-the loop simulation are within an arm's reach. OMNEST™ is also
an excellent platform for performing parallel distributed simulations or
doing research on parallel simulation.

OMNEST™ runs on Windows, Linux, and UNIX systems and since the source code
is provided, users have been using OMNEST™ on other platforms as well.
