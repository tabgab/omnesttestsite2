Infotek and Omnest Global sign Distribution Agreement

Taipei, August 21st 2004


Omnest Global Inc. is proud to announce its partnership with InfoTek Information Systems Co., Inc. of Taipei.


Infotek will be the primary contact for distribution and customer support within the Republic of China and Taiwan. The R.O.C. and Taiwan have been a hub for high-tech manufacturing and design for years. By working together, Infotek and Omnest Global will strive to provide high availability of local customer support and expertise, increasing turnover and visibility in this important market.

To celebrate the new partnership, Infotek and Omnest Global have joined forces to invite interested professionals to attend a workshop introducing the Omnest Simulation Environment (OMNEST™) in detail. In a separate event, Mr. Ahmet Sekercioglu. a Senior Lecturer at Electrical and Computer Systems Engineering Department of Monash University will also be presenting his work on IPv6 simulation with OMNEST™ at the IPv6 summit in Taiwan.

Details on these two events will be released shortly.
